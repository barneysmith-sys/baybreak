# Changelog

## Mobile release
- **Touch controls:** big Win98-style controls on phones and tablets only. Skating: ◀ ▶ BRK, DUCK, ACT and a round JUMP. Roadster: UP, DOWN and BOOST. Multi-touch, pressed states and light vibration where supported. The controls change with the level and hide during dialogs and cutscenes.
- **Phone layout:** the game window stays sharp at its original aspect ratio. In portrait, the controls sit below the window. In landscape, they sit in the side gutters and the taskbar steps aside. Controls never cover the play area, and iPhone safe areas are respected.
- **Phone start:** a big ▶ TAP TO PLAY on the title screen, and a non-blocking "rotate for a bigger view" tip in portrait.
- **Readability:** larger readable dialogs, a larger HUD and a full-screen X phone on mobile. No accidental scrolling or pinch-zoom while playing.
- **Desktop:** completely unchanged.

## Roadster correction
- The foreground suspension bridge is removed from the Roadster flight: its cables, deck, towers, steelwork, collision boxes and deck surface. The illustrated panorama, including the distant Bay Bridge, is unchanged. Drones, gulls, blimp, coins, timing and the autopilot popup are unchanged.

## Final release pass
- **Roadster flight:** rebuilt on the illustrated San Francisco panorama as separate sky and city layers (`assets/art/flight-sky.png`, `flight-city.png`): Oracle Park, downtown, Salesforce Tower, the Transamerica Pyramid, the Embarcadero, the Ferry Building and the Bay Bridge. The placeholder cream clock building, pier sheds and Ghirardelli are removed.
- **Corgi Stampede:** only the five illustrated corgi girls and their dogs, 39 jumpable pairs in a singles, doubles and triples rhythm. Cups, ice, spills, leashes and loose projectiles are removed.
- **Golden Gate Finale:** the sax player, drummer, music gear and notes are removed. The SF Giants billboard is the final jump.
- **New X cutscene:** the skater stops in the Presidio and posts about his AI SaaS from a dark-mode phone. Fictional replies roll in and the counters climb, then VIRAL MODE: LEVEL 05. POST works by mouse, touch or keyboard, with SKIP and no-input fallbacks. Nothing goes online.
- **Golden Gate characters:** illustrated dog walkers stand in the lane as obstacles, and the passive code-drawn spectators are removed.
- **Game-over window:** all button labels fit at every window size.

## Earlier in this update
- Four illustrated investors, illustrated Waymos at 80 × 28, roof-to-roof chains, and shorter investor and Waymo encounters.
- Unlimited continues from the last checkpoint, with a Win98 game-over window.
- San Francisco time clock, and the USNS Pacific Titan and Taiwan semiconductor ships in Level 03.
